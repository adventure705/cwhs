Ordering & Personalization

How does it work?
Personalization details are submitted after payment.
After purchase, you’ll receive access to the Order Form link where you can enter your personalized information.

I’ll create your first draft and send it to the email address provided in your order.

Please make sure to also check your Junk/Spam folder just in case.

Once you review and approve the draft, your final video will be delivered.

Can I use my own photos or videos?

Yes! Depending on the package and customization type, you may send photos, video clips, ultrasound images, or special memories.

Additional editing time may be required when incorporating personal photos or videos.

If additional editing fees are required, I’ll always let you know first and provide a separate payment link before any extra work is completed.

Can I request add-ons later?

Yes! Additional customization or add-ons can be purchased later if needed.

If an additional payment is required, a separate payment link will be sent together with your first draft video.

Can you create videos in other languages?

Yes! Many of my videos can be created in different languages, including Trip Reveal Videos, Gender Reveal Videos, Baby Announcements, Birthday Surprise Videos, and more.

You can view the currently available languages here:

Available Languages

Please note that some video styles may use specific fonts that do not support certain languages or characters. In those cases, a similar font may be used to ensure the text displays correctly.

If you don’t see the language you’re looking for, or if you have a specific request, please feel free to contact me through Chat with Us. I’ll be happy to check what is possible for your video.

Can I change specific clips from the sample video?

Yes! Specific clips from the sample videos can be changed or replaced.

Custom clip changes may require additional editing time and fees. If so, I’ll always let you know before proceeding.

Can I change the background music?

Yes! You’re welcome to request different background music

Depending on the selected music, the final video length may become slightly shorter.

If your requested music requires extending the video length or adding extra scenes, I’ll always discuss any additional cost with you first

Delivery & Processing

How long does delivery take?

The first draft is usually delivered within 24–48 hours for most orders.

In many cases, it may be delivered even sooner depending on the schedule.

Holiday seasons may take slightly longer.

Can I rush my order?

Yes! If you need your video urgently, please select Rush My Order when filling out the Order Form after purchase.

If you need it even faster than the stated 12–24 hour rush delivery time, please contact me through Chat with Us first. I’ll check whether your requested delivery time is possible and get back to you as quickly as possible.

I’ll do my very best to help deliver it within the time you need

How fast is Rush Delivery?

Rush orders are guaranteed to be delivered within 12–24 hours.

If you need it even faster than 12–24 hours, please contact me through Chat with Us before ordering.

Delivery time may vary depending on current workload, level of customization, and revision requests.

Are revisions included with Rush Orders?

Yes. Minor revisions are still included.

However, very large changes after delivery may require a little extra editing time.

Is there an additional fee for Rush Orders?

Yes. Because Rush Orders require priority scheduling and editing, an additional fee may apply. I’ll always explain the details clearly in advance.

Will I receive a preview before the final video?

Yes. A preview / first draft will always be shared before final delivery.

How will the final video be delivered?

Final videos are usually delivered via a Google Drive download link.

A simple guide on how to watch the video on your TV will also be included.

Revisions & Changes

Are revisions included?

Yes. Minor revisions are included before final delivery.

Examples include text corrections, pronunciation adjustments, and small edits.

Requests that require significant scene changes, additional customization, or extra editing time may incur an additional fee.

If additional fees are required, I’ll always explain them and provide a separate payment link before proceeding.

Can I completely change scenes after the draft is made?

Small adjustments are totally fine.

However, large scene replacements or major customization changes may require additional editing fees due to the time involved.

Downloads & Technical Help

Why didn’t I receive an instant download?

These videos are personalized/custom-made products, so the final video is created after your order information is submitted.

Can I see sample videos?

Yes! You can view sample videos here:

  • Trip Reveal Video Samples
  • Gender Reveal Video Samples
  • Baby Announcement Video Samples

If you don’t see the sample you’re looking for, or if you’re looking for a different theme, please contact me through Chat with Us. I’ll be happy to check what’s available for your request.

Can I watch the video on TV?

Yes! The final video will be delivered as an MP4 file.

You can upload the file to YouTube and watch it on your TV.

A detailed TV viewing guide will also be included together with your final delivery.

Can I share the video on social media?

Yes! However, upload availability may vary depending on the video and platform.

In some cases, music copyright restrictions may prevent direct uploads.

For Facebook and Instagram, I generally recommend sharing the video through a YouTube link rather than direct upload.

TikTok uploads usually work without issues.

Refunds & Cancellations

Do you offer refunds?

Because each video is created specifically for your order, refunds are not available once editing has started.

As these are digital products, refunds are not available once the final video has been delivered.

Instant Download products are also non-refundable and cannot be canceled once purchased.

If you have any questions before ordering, please feel free to contact me. I’m always happy to help clarify anything before you place your order.

Can I cancel my order?

Because personalized/custom videos are specially created for each order, cancellations or refunds may not be possible once editing has started.

Please also note that Instant Download products cannot be canceled once the order has been placed due to the digital nature of the product.
